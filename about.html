Nearly three decades of supporting business technology.

Established in 1997, ATAPI Systems is a B2B IT solutions provider based in Kochi, Kerala. We work with organisations to provide business computing, enterprise infrastructure and technology solutions suited to their requirements.

Our work spans business laptops, desktops and mini PCs, printing solutions, UPS systems, enterprise servers, pre-owned premium laptops and mobility solutions. We also provide enterprise security and networking, cloud and data storage solutions.

We serve government, education and corporate organisations, with customers across sectors including small and medium enterprises, healthcare, hospitality and FMCG.
